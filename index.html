Reframe Systems

reframe.systems · Co-founded 2022 · Feb 2023 – Present

We need to learn to love building again in the US. We are short on homes, power plants, data centers, and the factories that make all of it. Since 1970, US manufacturing productivity has grown about ninefold while construction productivity has fallen, roughly 1% a year. And about 41% of the construction workforce is expected to retire by 2031. Housing and public works will keep getting more expensive unless we change how we build.

We build software-defined factories that turn a design model into a finished building.

I co-founded Reframe in 2022 with Vikas Enti (CEO) and Aaron Small (Head of Operations and Digital AI). I lead Manufacturing, Software, and Robotics.

Every part we build starts as a released design model. Our software turns it into machine files, robot programs and work instructions, and schedules it on the line. Nobody redraws anything by hand. Our robotic cells frame wall panels every day in production.

FAB1 is our new factory in Billerica, MA. We got the keys, and 70 days later the first part came off the line (we had planned for 120). Total equipment cost was under $5M. And we designed it to be copied... the next one gets the same layout and the same software, close to wherever the next customer is.

Foxglove Actuate: Reframe Systems

Housing first

Housing is the first product. 10 homes delivered so far, from ADUs to triple-deckers, and 114 more units in the pipeline.

None of this is specific to housing. A wall panel is the same job whether it ends up in an apartment or in workforce housing next to a new power plant. The model-to-factory pipeline also works for the buildings behind energy and data centers, and for the carts and fixtures the factory itself runs on. That is where this goes next.
